# Focaccia 

This recipe can be warm or cold fermented depending on time constraints. The quantities suit a baking dish of about a X b cm.

## Ingredients
[] 500g bread flour (I use Lauche Wallaby flour as it has the highest protein level of any of the flour varieties available from the supermarket)
[] 375g water (this is on the high side - you could safely reduce this to about 330g)
